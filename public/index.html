Hi, I'm Quang.

I'm an electrical engineering student at San José State with a lot of software behind me. I chose EE because it lets my code leave the screen: the firmware I write runs on boards I wire, so a product ends up working exactly the way I pictured it.

I want a career in embedded systems, designing and building products from my own ideas, from the circuit up through the firmware and the interface. Right now I work as a manufacturing technician at Intel in Santa Clara, in a Class 10 cleanroom, after four years managing a Panda Express in San Jose.

Most of what I do for fun is strategy. I like games where you build the thing you win with, and then have to out-think the other side to use it.

What I play

  • Trading card games

    Yu-Gi-Oh!, Pokémon and One Piece. Deck building is the part I love: one plan, a fixed number of slots, and every card has to earn its place.

  • Tiến Lên (13)

    The Vietnamese card game everyone calls 13. Thirteen cards each, and the whole game is reading the table: when to break a run, when to pass, and when to spend your 2s.

  • Badminton

    Strategy at full speed. Know your own strengths, find the other player's weak side, and keep pressing it until the rally is yours.

  • Building things

    There's usually an ESP32 on my desk with wires coming out of it. Lately: a card scanner, an RPM sensor, and a 3D-printed relief-map puzzle.
